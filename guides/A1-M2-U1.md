## A1-M2-U1 Nouns and articles

Goal: Name things with a/an or the, and distinguish one from more than one.

Prerequisites: Essere and basic adjective agreement. This unit has three practice passes; do not learn every article at once.

### Vocabulary preview

- Active nouns: The article shows gender
- the second form shows the plural.

- Singular — Plural — Meaning
- il libro — i libri — book
- la casa — le case — house or home
- la macchina — le macchine — car
- l'appartamento, masculine — gli appartamenti — apartment
- il cane — i cani — dog
- la porta — le porte — door
- lo studente — gli studenti — male student
- la studentessa — le studentesse — female student
- l'amico, masculine — gli amici — male friend
- l'amica, feminine — le amiche — female friend

- Second pass, active adjectives: grande → grandi, big
- piccolo/piccola → piccoli/piccole, small
- bello/bella → belli/belle, beautiful or nice. Put these after the noun in this unit. We will study other adjective positions later.

Recognition exceptions: il problema → i problemi, problem; la mano → le mani, hand; la città → le città, city; il caffè → i caffè, coffee. They illustrate why the ending alone is not enough to identify gender or plural.

### Pass 1 Gender and a or an

Every noun has grammatical gender. Many -o nouns are masculine and many -a nouns feminine; -e nouns can be either. Learn a noun with its article. Gender is a grammatical category; it does not imply that a house or book has a natural sex.

- Article — Use — Example
- un — most masculine nouns, including before a vowel — un libro; un appartamento
- uno — masculine before s + consonant, z, gn, ps and some other uncommon beginnings — uno studente
- una — feminine before a consonant — una casa
- un' — feminine before a vowel — un'amica

There is no apostrophe in un amico. There is one in un'amica because feminine una loses its final a before the vowel.

### Pass 2 The and description

- Article — Use — Example
- il — most masculine singular nouns beginning with a consonant — il libro
- lo — masculine singular with the special beginnings used after uno — lo studente
- l' — masculine or feminine singular before a vowel — l'amico; l'amica
- la — feminine singular before a consonant — la casa

Il libro è piccolo. The book is small. La casa è grande. The house is big. You can also place a simple adjective after the noun: una casa grande, a big house.

### Pass 3 Plurals

- Common pattern — Example
- masculine -o becomes -i — libro → libri
- feminine -a becomes -e — casa → case
- -e becomes -i in either gender — cane → cani; studente → studenti
- feminine -ca becomes -che — amica → amiche
- some words need an individually learned plural — amico → amici
- many words ending in an accented vowel do not change — città → città

Definite article plurals: il → i; lo → gli; masculine l' → gli; la and feminine l' → le. There is no direct plural equivalent of un/una here; “some” is introduced later. For now use definite plurals or bare plural nouns where the pattern explicitly permits them.

### Examples and communication

- È un libro. It is a book.
- La macchina è bella. The car is nice.
- Gli appartamenti sono piccoli. The apartments are small.
- Le amiche sono italiane. The female friends are Italian.

— È una casa?
— Sì, è una casa. È grande.

Common mistake: Agreement follows the noun: le case piccole, not le case piccolo. Do not add -s to make an Italian plural.

Quick reference: Learn article + noun + plural together. Adjectives agree with the noun they describe.

### Ready for audio practice

- Type — Prompt — Model

- Integration — The female friends are French, but the male student is Italian. — Le amiche sono francesi, ma lo studente è italiano.
