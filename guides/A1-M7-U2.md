## A1-M7-U2 Shopping and prices

Goal: Ask the price, choose an item, and ask how to pay.

Prerequisites: Numbers, food quantities, articles, present verbs, potere. Shopping pronouns are restricted chunks in this unit.

### Vocabulary preview

- Active first pass: comprare, buy, regular -are
- costare, cost, regular -are
- pagare, pay
- il negozio → i negozi, shop
- l'euro → gli euro, euro, unchanged noun
- la carta → le carte, card
- in contanti, in cash
- troppo, too, before an adjective
- caro/cara → cari/care, expensive
- quanto costa?, how much does it cost?
- quanto costano?, how much do they cost?

- Active second pass: lo scontrino → gli scontrini, receipt
- la ricevuta → le ricevute, receipt
- lo sconto → gli sconti, discount
- la taglia → le taglie, clothing size
- il numero → i numeri, number or shoe size
- la maglietta → le magliette, T-shirt
- la scarpa → le scarpe, shoe
- questo/questa → questi/queste, this/these.

- Active selection chunks: Lo prendo, I'll take it, masculine singular item
- La prendo, I'll take it, feminine singular item. Recognition: Ha qualcosa di meno caro?, do you have something cheaper?, polite
- Che taglia porta?, what size do you wear?, polite. These contain additional constructions and are not generative practice targets here.

### The patterns

Comprare: compro, compri, compra, compriamo, comprate, comprano. Costare: costo, costi, costa, costiamo, costate, costano; the main price forms are costa, it costs, and costano, they cost.

Pagare keeps the hard g with h before i: pago, paghi, paga, paghiamo, pagate, pagano. Its spelling is introduced before any first-person plural or informal question is required.

- Quanto costa il biglietto? How much does the ticket cost?
- Quanto costano le mele? How much do the apples cost?
- Il biglietto costa dieci euro. The ticket costs ten euros.
- Le scarpe costano cinquanta euro. The shoes cost fifty euros.

For prices, tre euro e cinquanta means €3.50, with cents understood. Tre euro e cinque means €3.05. Price prompts should specify an amount unambiguously, preferably as a numeral. Sono tre euro e cinquanta gives a total: that is three euros fifty.

Troppo stays unchanged as “too” before caro: La maglietta è troppo cara, the T-shirt is too expensive; Le scarpe sono troppo care, the shoes are too expensive. Caro agrees with the noun.

Use questo/questa/questi/queste + noun without a definite article in our models: questo libro, this book; questa maglietta, this T-shirt; queste scarpe, these shoes. Other this/that patterns come later.

### Selection and payment

Lo prendo and La prendo are labelled chunks containing a direct-object pronoun. Choose the chunk according to the item's gender. Their full pronoun system, plural forms, and other verb combinations belong to A2. Do not introduce lo voglio, li compro, or pronoun attachment by analogy in this unit.

Posso pagare con la carta? Can I pay by card? Posso pagare in contanti? Can I pay in cash? These use known potere + infinitive, with the payment expressions explicitly introduced above.

### Examples and communication

- Compro due mele. I buy two apples.
- Questa maglietta è bella. This T-shirt is nice.
- Posso avere lo scontrino? May I have the receipt?

— Quanto costa questa maglietta?
— Costa venti euro.
— La prendo. Posso pagare con la carta?
— Sì.

Quick reference: Singular item costa; plural items costano. Euro does not change. Lo/la prendo are limited selection chunks.

### Ready for audio practice

Pass 1: prices and payment with familiar goods. Pass 2: shopping nouns and this/these. Pass 3: singular selection chunks. Numbers and item combinations should vary.

- Type — Prompt — Model
- Core — How much does the ticket cost? — Quanto costa il biglietto?
- Variation — These shoes are too expensive. — Queste scarpe sono troppo care.
- Integration — I want to buy two tickets. Can I pay by card? — Voglio comprare due biglietti. Posso pagare con la carta?
