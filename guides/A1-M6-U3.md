## A1-M6-U3 Transport and directions

Goal: Ask about travel, use basic transport language, and recognise polite directions.

Prerequisites: Movement, place prepositions, clock time, modals and partire.

### Vocabulary preview

Active pass 1:

- Singular — Plural — Meaning
- l'autobus, masculine — gli autobus — bus
- il treno — i treni — train
- la macchina — le macchine — car, already introduced
- la bicicletta — le biciclette — bicycle
- il biglietto — i biglietti — ticket
- il binario — i binari — track; track number used to locate a train
- l'orario, masculine — gli orari — timetable/schedule

- Active: arrivare, arrive, regular -are
- in autobus / in treno / in macchina / in bicicletta, by bus/train/car/bicycle
- a piedi, on foot
- per, for/to in a ticket or train destination
- da … a …, from … to …
- ripetere, already introduced as an infinitive.

Pass 2 direction chunks: Vada dritto, go straight ahead, polite singular; Giri a destra, turn right; Giri a sinistra, turn left; Prenda la prima via a destra, take the first street on the right; Prenda la seconda via a sinistra, take the second street on the left; Attraversi la piazza, cross the square. La via → le vie, street; prima/seconda, first/second, feminine here, are introduced inside these chunks.

Recognition: il pullman, coach; la metropolitana / la metro, underground/metro; il tram, tram; il taxi, taxi; la moto, motorbike; l'aereo, plane; la nave, ship; il traghetto, ferry; perdere, miss/lose. Do not require their production without an extension preview.

### The patterns

Transport method commonly uses in without an article: Vado in treno. I go by train. Taking a particular transport uses prendere + article + noun: Prendo il treno. I take the train.

Un biglietto per Roma, a ticket to Rome, introduces per with a destination. Il treno per Milano, the train to Milan, uses the same pattern. Departure and arrival reuse the present: Il treno parte alle otto e arriva alle dieci. The train leaves at eight and arrives at ten.

Route: da Roma a Milano, from Rome to Milan. With a definite-article place, da joins it: dalla stazione al museo, from the station to the museum. For track information, learn Il treno parte dal binario tre, the train departs from track three. English may use “platform three”, but binario literally identifies the track.

Distance chunk: È a dieci minuti a piedi, it is ten minutes away on foot. Substitute only a known number in È a [number] minuti a piedi. This is a taught distance pattern, not a licence to invent all measure expressions.

### Direction grammar preview

Vada, giri, prenda, attraversi are polite command forms. The command system is taught at A2. Here the direction expressions are introduced openly as chunks. Recognition comes first; if you practise giving a direction now, use the exact chunk or the explicitly previewed right/left variant. Do not derive commands from arbitrary verbs.

### Examples and communication

- Andiamo a Roma in treno. We are going to Rome by train.
- A che ora parte il treno? What time does the train leave?
- Mi scusi, dov'è la stazione? Excuse me, where is the station? polite.

— Mi scusi, dov'è la stazione?
— Vada dritto. Giri a destra.
— Non capisco. Può ripetere?
— Vada dritto. Giri a destra.

Quick reference: Method: in treno. Taking it: prendere il treno. Destination: per Roma. Directions are labelled command chunks.

### Ready for audio practice

Pass 1 builds travel sentences. Pass 2 matches heard directions to meanings, then optionally retrieves the introduced chunks. Do not mix unfamiliar command forms into ordinary present-tense practice.

- Type — Prompt — Model
- Core — I take the bus. — Prendo l'autobus.
- Variation — The train leaves from track two at nine. — Il treno parte dal binario due alle nove.
- Integration — We have to go from the station to the museum on foot. — Dobbiamo andare dalla stazione al museo a piedi.
