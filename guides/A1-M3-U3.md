## A1-M3-U3 Essential irregular verbs

Goal: Talk about common actions, movement, well-being, knowledge, and drinking.

Prerequisites: Regular verbs, location patterns, possession, articles.

### Vocabulary preview

Do this unit in three passes. The forms are a reference, not one large first-session drill.

- Pass — Active language — Meaning or taught pattern
- 1 — fare; la colazione; la doccia → le docce; il lavoro → i lavori; la scuola → le scuole — do/make; breakfast; shower; work/job; school
- 1 — fare colazione; fare la doccia; andare; andare a casa; andare a scuola; andare al lavoro — have breakfast; take a shower; go; go home; go to school; go to work
- 2 — stare; venire; da — be/stay in particular expressions; come; from in movement
- 2 — stare bene; stare male; venire da + city; venire con + person — be well; be unwell; come from; come with
- 3 — sapere; bere; il latte — know/know how; drink; milk, mass noun

Al lavoro is introduced as the fixed destination in andare al lavoro. Its article combination is explained in A1-M6-U2. It does not yet license every al/alla combination. Che lavoro fai? What work do you do? is now an active question built with fare, rather than an unexplained identity example.

### Forms

- Person — Fare — Andare — Stare — Venire — Sapere — Bere
- io — faccio — vado — sto — vengo — so — bevo
- tu — fai — vai — stai — vieni — sai — bevi
- lui/lei/Lei — fa — va — sta — viene — sa — beve
- noi — facciamo — andiamo — stiamo — veniamo — sappiamo — beviamo
- voi — fate — andate — state — venite — sapete — bevete
- loro — fanno — vanno — stanno — vengono — sanno — bevono

### The patterns

Fare needs the appropriate activity expression: Faccio colazione, but Faccio la doccia. Learn the phrase with its article choice.

Andare expresses going; venire expresses coming toward a relevant person or place. Reuse a + city and in + country: Vado a Roma, I am going to Rome; Veniamo in Italia, we are coming to Italy. A prompt about come/go should establish the speaker's viewpoint.

Stare is not interchangeable with essere everywhere. Use sto bene / sto male for well-being in this unit. Ordinary location remains sono a Roma. In stare bene/male, the adverb does not change with gender.

- Sapere + infinitive expresses a learned skill: So leggere, I know how to read. No linking preposition is needed. Non so means I don't know and is an active short response. Knowledge of people, such as “I know Maria”, uses a different verb developed later
- do not use sapere for that example.

Bere + drink: Bevo il latte, I drink the milk; Bevo un caffè, I drink a coffee. Learn its forms without trying to create them from a regular -ere stem.

### Examples and communication

- Fate colazione? Are you plural having breakfast?
- Mia sorella va a scuola. My sister goes to school.
- Vieni con Maria? Are you coming with Maria?
- Sappiamo scrivere. We know how to write.
- Che lavoro fai? — Sono insegnante. What do you do? — I am a teacher.

Common mistakes: Vado, not ando; faccio, not fo as the course's neutral model. Use sto bene, not sono bene.

Quick reference: Learn useful expressions alongside irregular forms. Sapere + infinitive needs no preposition.

### Ready for audio practice

Pass 1: fare and andare. Pass 2: stare and venire. Pass 3: sapere and bere. Expand from io/tu to all people, then mix with regular verbs.

- Type — Prompt — Model
- Core — We go home. — Andiamo a casa.
- Variation — Can you read, meaning learned skill, informal singular? — Sai leggere?
- Integration — My mother has breakfast and drinks a coffee. — Mia madre fa colazione e beve un caffè.
