## A1-M7-U3 Polite requests

Goal: Make a polite request and handle a short service encounter using the language already learned.

Prerequisites: Food, quantities, prices, noun phrases, modals, greetings.

### Vocabulary preview

- Active chunk: vorrei, I would like. It can be followed by a noun phrase or a known infinitive: Vorrei un caffè
- Vorrei comprare un biglietto. This is a specific useful form of volere, not a regular present-tense ending.

- Active chunks: Ecco, here is/here are, used when handing something over
- Ecco a Lei, here you are, polite
- solo, only, before the requested noun phrase
- basta così, that's all. Il servizio, service, appears only in the recognition question below.

Recognition service language: Desidera? What would you like? polite; Subito, right away; Vuole qualcos'altro? Would you like anything else? polite; È incluso il servizio? Is service included? The last question includes a form not otherwise taught here and stays recognition-only. Do not require it in variable production.

### The pattern

Vorrei + noun: Vorrei un cappuccino, I would like a cappuccino. Vorrei + infinitive: Vorrei pagare, I would like to pay. You may add per favore.

Vorrei belongs to the conditional system, which is taught later. In this unit it is an active chunk with two clearly defined slots. Do not generate other conditional persons from it.

Posso avere …? provides a second polite request pattern based on the present modal already learned. Voglio … remains useful for talking about desires, but vorrei is a courteous default when asking staff for something.

The staff question Vuole …? uses the third-person singular present of volere for formal Lei. The English translation “Would you like …?” does not mean that every such Italian staff question uses a conditional.

### Examples and communication

- Vorrei una bottiglia d'acqua, per favore. I would like a bottle of water, please.
- Vorrei comprare due biglietti per Roma. I would like to buy two tickets to Rome.
- Solo il conto, per favore. Just the bill, please.

Complete service example; staff recognition phrases have been previewed above:

Staff: Buongiorno! Desidera?
Learner: Buongiorno. Vorrei un cappuccino e un cornetto, per favore.
Staff: Subito. Vuole qualcos'altro?
Learner: No, grazie. Basta così.
Staff: Sono tre euro e cinquanta.
Learner: Posso pagare con la carta?
Staff: Sì.
Learner: Grazie.
Staff: Prego. Arrivederci.

- Meaning of the staff turns: Good morning! What would you like? / Right away. Anything else? / That is €3.50 / Yes / You're welcome. Goodbye. The learner's turns use active language
- the staff turns may be heard without becoming production requirements.

Common mistake: Vorrei pagare, not vorrei pago. Vorrei does not change to agree with the requested noun.

Quick reference: Vorrei + noun phrase or infinitive. Treat it as one polite first-person form until the conditional is taught.

### Ready for audio practice

Vary the food, amount, ticket destination, and payment method. Then combine two or three short turns. Keep the learner's production within active language even when the staff audio contains recognition material.

- Type — Prompt — Model
- Core — I would like a tea, please. — Vorrei un tè, per favore.
- Variation — I would like half a kilogram of bread. — Vorrei mezzo chilo di pane.
- Integration — I would like two tickets to Milan. How much do they cost? — Vorrei due biglietti per Milano. Quanto costano?
