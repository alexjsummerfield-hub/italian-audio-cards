## A1-M5-U2 Piacere

Goal: Say what people like and ask about preferences.

Prerequisites: Nouns, articles, singular/plural, infinitives, negation.

### Vocabulary preview

- Active first pass: mi piace, I like
- ti piace, you informal like
- mi piacciono, I like plural things. La musica and il caffè are already active.

- Active leisure nouns: lo sport, sport, unchanged in plural
- il film → i film, film/movie
- il calcio, football/soccer
- la chitarra → le chitarre, guitar. Active infinitives: viaggiare, travel
- ballare, dance
- cucinare, cook. In this unit these infinitives can follow piace
- independent present forms can be activated using the regular -are pattern in a later pass.

- Second pass: the remaining person forms in the table below
- a + person, indicating who likes something in this pattern. Giocare a calcio, play football, and suonare la chitarra, play the guitar, are active infinitive expressions after piace. The prepositions/articles belong to those expressions
- do not substitute arbitrary activities yet.

Recognition: la lettura, reading; la cucina, cooking or kitchen; la fotografia, photography; per niente, at all, as in Non mi piace per niente, I don't like it at all.

### The pattern

Begin from Mi piace X = X is pleasing to me → I like X. The thing liked controls piace or piacciono. The person who likes it does not.

- Person who likes — Form — Example
- I — mi — Mi piace il caffè.
- you, informal singular — ti — Ti piace il caffè?
- he — gli — Gli piace il caffè.
- she; polite singular you — le / Le — Le piace il caffè.
- we — ci — Ci piace il caffè.
- you, plural — vi — Vi piace il caffè?
- they — a loro — A loro piace il caffè.

Formal Le can address a man or woman. Lowercase le can mean to her; context distinguishes them. These pronouns are introduced for piacere only; the full indirect-object system comes later. A loro is the clear beginner model for “they like”. You may encounter gli for “to them” in speech, but it is not required here.

Use piace for a singular thing or an infinitive activity:

- Mi piace la musica. I like music.
- Mi piace leggere. I like reading.

Use piacciono for plural things:

- Mi piacciono i film. I like films.
- Ci piacciono i libri. We like books.

General likes commonly use the definite article: il caffè, la musica, i film. Infinitives used as activities do not need it: leggere, viaggiare.

Name the person with a: A Maria piace leggere. Maria likes reading. Put non before the pronoun pattern: Non mi piace il tè. I do not like tea. With a named person: A Maria non piace il tè.

### Examples and communication

- Ti piace cucinare? Do you like cooking?
- Le piacciono i libri. She likes books, or you formal like books.
- A Marco piace giocare a calcio. Marco likes playing football.

— Ti piace il caffè?
— Sì, mi piace il caffè, ma preferisco il tè.

Common mistake: Mi piacciono i film, not mi piace i film. Do not create io piaccio il caffè by following English word order.

Quick reference: Person marker + piace + singular/infinitive; person marker + piacciono + plural.

### Ready for audio practice

Start with mi/ti and singular things. Add plural things, then infinitives, then other people. Distinguish liking from preferring.

- Type — Prompt — Model
- Core — I like music. — Mi piace la musica.
- Variation — Do you like films, plural you? — Vi piacciono i film?
- Integration — My sister likes reading, but she prefers dancing. — A mia sorella piace leggere, ma preferisce ballare.
