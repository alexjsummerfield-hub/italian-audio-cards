# A2-M3-U2 · Completed obligations and possibilities

Complete the earlier A0/A1 units and preceding A2 units before cumulative practice. This is an A2 testing sample, not a complete A2 syllabus.

## Preparation

Prerequisites include both passato prossimo units and present modals. Use the present auxiliary + dovuto/potuto/voluto + infinitive. These cards describe completed events; an ongoing past desire or obligation may instead require imperfetto in another context.

- Dovere → dovuto — have to → had to
- Potere → potuto — be able to → was/were able to
- Volere → voluto — want → wanted/chose

With an avere infinitive, use avere: ho dovuto lavorare, I had to work; abbiamo potuto leggere, we were able to read. Our initial model follows the following infinitive's auxiliary: Marco è dovuto andare; Anna è dovuta partire. With essere, the participle agrees with the subject. An avere alternative such as Marco ha dovuto andare is also accepted; it is used in contemporary Italian, not automatically an error. Reflexive-pronoun placement introduces further rules, so past modal + reflexive is not tested here.
