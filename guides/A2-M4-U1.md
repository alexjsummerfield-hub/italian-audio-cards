# A2-M4-U1 · Health and how you feel

Complete the earlier A0/A1 units and preceding A2 units before cumulative practice. This is an A2 testing sample, not a complete A2 syllabus.

## Preparation

These are language practice expressions for describing symptoms, not instructions about treatment. Begin with avere + the expressions below; then use the explicitly introduced fa/fanno male and sentirsi patterns.

- La testa — head
- La schiena — back
- La gola — throat
- Il dente → i denti — tooth → teeth
- Il piede → i piedi — foot → feet
- La febbre — fever
- Il raffreddore — cold (illness)
- Avere mal di testa — have a headache
- Avere mal di gola — have a sore throat
- Avere mal di denti — have a toothache
- Avere la febbre — have a fever
- Avere il raffreddore — have a cold
- Mi fa male la schiena — my back hurts
- Mi fanno male i piedi — my feet hurt
- Sentirsi: mi sento, ti senti, si sente, ci sentiamo, vi sentite, si sentono
- Non mi sento bene — I do not feel well
- Il medico — doctor, already introduced in A1
- Da due giorni — for two days, continuing into the present

Italian uses the present for an ongoing symptom with da: ho mal di gola da due giorni, I have had a sore throat for two days. With fa male, a singular body part controls fa; plural body parts control fanno. Di becomes mal di in the complete symptom expressions above; learn those expressions together.
