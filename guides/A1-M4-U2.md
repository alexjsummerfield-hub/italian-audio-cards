## A1-M4-U2 Reflexive routines

Goal: Describe waking, getting up, washing, dressing, relaxing, and falling asleep.

Prerequisites: Regular present verbs, fare and andare, time and frequency.

### Vocabulary preview

- Active pass 1: svegliarsi, wake up
- alzarsi, get up
- lavarsi, wash oneself. Pronoun forms: mi, ti, si, ci, vi, si, matched to the people below.

- Active pass 2: vestirsi, get dressed
- rilassarsi, relax
- addormentarsi, fall asleep
- il letto → i letti, bed
- andare a letto, go to bed.

Recognition extensions: riposarsi, rest; pranzare, have lunch; cenare, have dinner; guardare la TV, watch TV; guardare un film, watch a film. Their optional activation is covered in Appendix B. They are not needed for the first routine practice.

### The pattern

In these verbs, the pronoun belongs to the verb pattern. It changes with the person. Mi alzo means I get up; ti alzi means you get up. Put non before the pronoun: Non mi alzo presto. I do not get up early.

- Person — Pronoun — Alzarsi — Vestirsi
- io — mi — mi alzo — mi vesto
- tu — ti — ti alzi — ti vesti
- lui/lei/Lei — si — si alza — si veste
- noi — ci — ci alziamo — ci vestiamo
- voi — vi — vi alzate — vi vestite
- loro — si — si alzano — si vestono

For the -are reflexives, conjugate the verb as before and add the matching pronoun. Svegliarsi: mi sveglio, ti svegli, si sveglia, ci svegliamo, vi svegliate, si svegliano. Note the single i in ti svegli and ci svegliamo. Rilassarsi: mi rilasso, ti rilassi, si rilassa, ci rilassiamo, vi rilassate, si rilassano. Addormentarsi: mi addormento, ti addormenti, si addormenta, ci addormentiamo, vi addormentate, si addormentano. Lavarsi: mi lavo, ti lavi, si lava, ci laviamo, vi lavate, si lavano.

Not every Italian verb with this pattern translates naturally with “myself”. Treat rilassarsi as relax and addormentarsi as fall asleep. Dormire, sleep, has no reflexive pronoun in our pattern. Falling asleep and sleeping are different actions.

### Examples and communication

- Mi sveglio alle sette. I wake up at seven.
- Ci alziamo presto. We get up early.
- Maria si veste. Maria gets dressed.
- Non si addormentano presto. They do not fall asleep early.

A routine using only introduced material:

Di solito mi sveglio alle sette. Mi alzo e faccio la doccia. Poi faccio colazione: bevo un caffè e mangio il pane. Vado al lavoro. Lavoro dalle nove alle cinque. La sera leggo un libro. Vado a letto verso le undici.

Usually I wake up at seven. I get up and take a shower. Then I have breakfast: I drink a coffee and eat the bread. I go to work. I work from nine to five. In the evening I read a book. I go to bed around eleven.

This is a model of connected speech, not a script to memorise. Change the times, person, and known activities in practice.

Common mistake: Ci alziamo, not mi alziamo. The pronoun and verb ending must identify the same person.

Quick reference: non + matching pronoun + conjugated verb. Learn each reflexive infinitive with its -si ending.

### Ready for audio practice

Use three verbs first. Add the remaining three, then combine with clock time and familiar non-reflexive activities. Modal + reflexive combinations are introduced separately next.

- Type — Prompt — Model
- Core — I get up at eight. — Mi alzo alle otto.
- Variation — Do you get dressed early, plural? — Vi vestite presto?
- Integration — My sister wakes up at seven and goes to school. — Mia sorella si sveglia alle sette e va a scuola.
