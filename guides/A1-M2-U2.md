## A1-M2-U2 Avere and possession

Goal: Say what people have, ask about family, and give an age.

Prerequisites: Articles, plurals, essere, questions and negation.

### Vocabulary preview

First pass: possessions from U1. Add tempo, time, in the article-free pattern avere tempo, have time.

Second pass, active family vocabulary:

- Singular — Plural — Meaning
- il padre — i padri — father
- la madre — le madri — mother
- il fratello — i fratelli — brother
- la sorella — le sorelle — sister
- il figlio — i figli — son; plural can mean children
- la figlia — le figlie — daughter
- il nonno — i nonni — grandfather; i nonni can mean grandparents
- la nonna — le nonne — grandmother
- il marito — i mariti — husband
- la moglie — le mogli — wife

- Third pass, active numbers: 0 zero, 1 uno, 2 due, 3 tre, 4 quattro, 5 cinque, 6 sei, 7 sette, 8 otto, 9 nove, 10 dieci
- 20 venti, 30 trenta, 40 quaranta, 50 cinquanta, 60 sessanta. L'anno → gli anni, year. More numbers follow in A1-M4-U1
- begin age practice with the numbers previewed here.

### The pattern

- Person — Avere — Meaning
- io — ho — I have
- tu — hai — you have
- lui/lei/Lei — ha — he/she has; you formal have
- noi — abbiamo — we have
- voi — avete — you plural have
- loro — hanno — they have

The h is silent: ho, hai, ha, hanno. It matters in writing.

Use avere + noun for possessions and family: Ho una macchina. I have a car. Abbiamo due figli. We have two children. A number replaces a/an: due libri, two books, not due un libro.

In general questions and negatives, plural family nouns can be article-free: Hai fratelli? Do you have brothers? Non ho sorelle. I do not have sisters. Ho tempo. I have time, also uses no article in this basic pattern.

Age uses avere: Ho trenta anni, normally contracted to Ho trent'anni, I am thirty years old. Both forms are understandable; practise the natural contracted form. Other previewed contractions: vent'anni, quarant'anni, cinquant'anni, sessant'anni. Quanti anni hai? How old are you? introduces quanti how many, masculine plural, in this age question. Quanti anni ha? is polite singular or about he/she.

### Examples and communication

- Maria ha un appartamento a Roma. Maria has an apartment in Rome.
- Non abbiamo una macchina. We do not have a car.
- Avete tre figli? Do you plural have three children?
- Ho quarant'anni. I am forty years old.

— Hai fratelli?
— Sì, ho un fratello e due sorelle. E tu?
— Non ho fratelli.

Common mistake: Ho trent'anni, not Sono trent'anni. Family nouns here name the relationship; the possessive system follows next.

Quick reference: Possession and age use avere. Non stays before the verb. Number + plural noun needs no indefinite article.

### Ready for audio practice

Pass 1: possessions and tempo. Pass 2: a small family set, then the rest. Pass 3: numbers and age. Do not mix all three sets on the first attempt.

- Type — Prompt — Model
- Core — We have a dog. — Abbiamo un cane.
- Variation — Do you have time, formal singular? — Ha tempo?
- Integration — I am thirty years old and have two daughters. — Ho trent'anni e ho due figlie.

## Original prototype vocabulary refresh

# A1–M2–U2 · Preparation

Read this before practice. These are the words and patterns used in the sample cards.

## Avere — to have

- Io ho — I have
- Tu hai — you have (one person, informal)
- Lui ha — he has
- Lei ha — she has
- Noi abbiamo — we have
- Voi avete — you have (several people)
- Loro hanno — they have

Italian usually omits the subject pronoun: Ho un libro means I have a book. The h in avere forms is silent.

### Negatives and questions

- Non ho un libro. — I do not have a book.
- Hai un libro? — Do you have a book?

Put non before the verb. Yes/no questions keep the same word order, with rising intonation. Formal Lei is not practised in this sample.

## Everyday nouns

- Un libro — a book
- Due libri — two books
- Una macchina — a car
- Due macchine — two cars
- Una casa — a house
- Due case — two houses

## Family

- Un fratello — a brother
- Due fratelli — two brothers
- Una sorella — a sister
- Due sorelle — two sisters
- Un figlio — a child / a son
- Due figli — two children / two sons
- Hai figli? — Do you have children?

## Quantities and time

- Due — two
- Tre — three
- Tempo — time
- Ho tempo. — I have time.
- Hai tempo? — Do you have time?
- Non ho tempo. — I do not have time.

These expressions with tempo do not use an article.

## Essere — to be

- Io sono — I am
- Tu sei — you are (one person, informal)
- Lui / lei è — he / she is
- Noi siamo — we are
- Voi siete — you are (several people)
- Loro sono — they are
- Non sono italiano. — I am not Italian (a man speaking).

### Useful words and chunks

- A casa — at home
- Siamo a casa. — We are at home.
- E — and
- È — is

## Articles and plurals

- Il libro — the book
- La casa — the house
- Un libro — a book
- Una casa — a house

In these examples, masculine noun plurals end in -i and feminine noun plurals end in -e.

## Adjectives

- Grande — big
- Piccolo / piccola — small (masculine / feminine)
- Nuovo / nuova — new (masculine / feminine)
- Italiano / italiana — Italian (masculine / feminine singular)
- Italiani / italiane — Italian (masculine / feminine plural)

Match the noun's gender and number. Adjectives follow nouns in these examples.

- Una casa piccola — a small house
- Un libro nuovo — a new book
- Due libri italiani — two Italian books
