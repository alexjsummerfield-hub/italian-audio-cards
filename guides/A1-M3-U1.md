## A1-M3-U1 Regular are verbs

Goal: Say what people do, where they live, and what they study or listen to.

Prerequisites: People, noun phrases, negation, a + city, in + country.

### Vocabulary preview

- Active first pass: parlare speak
- lavorare work
- studiare study
- abitare live/reside. Italiano and francese can also name the languages in parlare italiano, speak Italian, and studiare francese, study French. Oggi today
- bene well
- con with
- che cosa / cosa what.

- Second pass: mangiare eat
- ascoltare listen to
- il pane, bread, a mass noun in our examples
- la musica, music, also a mass noun. Both noun forms are active. Broader food vocabulary comes later.

### The pattern

The dictionary form, or infinitive, ends in -are. Remove -are and add the present ending. Learn the pattern with parlare:

- Person — Ending — Parlare
- io — -o — parlo
- tu — -i — parli
- lui/lei/Lei — -a — parla
- noi — -iamo — parliamo
- voi — -ate — parlate
- loro — -ano — parlano

The same pattern gives lavoro, lavori, lavora, lavoriamo, lavorate, lavorano and abito, abiti, abita, abitiamo, abitate, abitano.

For studiare, learn studio, studi, studia, studiamo, studiate, studiano. For mangiare, learn mangio, mangi, mangia, mangiamo, mangiate, mangiano. Do not create studii, mangii, or mangiiamo by adding an extra i. Ascoltare is regular: ascolto, ascolti, ascolta, ascoltiamo, ascoltate, ascoltano.

Italian present forms can cover English simple and continuous present: Studio italiano can mean I study Italian or I am studying Italian, depending on context. There is no need to translate every English “am … ing” with essere.

Patterns to practise:

- parlare/studiare + language: Parlo italiano.
- abitare + a city / in country: Abitiamo a Roma.
- parlare con + person: Parlo con Maria.
- ascoltare + direct object: Ascolto la musica. Italian does not add a separate word for English “to” here.
- mangiare + food: Mangio il pane. The article identifies the bread in this example.

### Examples and communication

- Lavori oggi? Are you working today?
- Maria non parla francese. Maria does not speak French.
- Che cosa studi? What do you study?
- Studiamo italiano. We study Italian.

— Dove abiti?
— Abito a Sydney. E tu?
— Abito a Roma. Studi italiano?
— Sì, studio italiano.

Common mistake: Non lavoro, not an added Italian equivalent of “do not” plus an infinitive. The person ending belongs on the main verb.

Quick reference: Stem + o, i, a, iamo, ate, ano. Non + present verb. One present form can express an ongoing activity.

### Ready for audio practice

Start with four verbs and singular subjects. Add plural forms, then mangiare and ascoltare. Ask what or where only with patterns introduced here.

- Type — Prompt — Model
- Core — I speak Italian. — Parlo italiano.
- Variation — What do you study, plural? — Che cosa studiate?
- Integration — My sister lives in Rome and studies French. — Mia sorella abita a Roma e studia francese.
