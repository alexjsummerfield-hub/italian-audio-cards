# A2-M2-U1 · Describing people

Complete the earlier A0/A1 units and preceding A2 units before cumulative practice. This is an A2 testing sample, not a complete A2 syllabus.

## Preparation

Descriptions use essere + adjective, with gender and number agreement. Hair and eye descriptions use avere + definite article + plural noun + adjective. These are descriptions, not literal word-for-word copies of English article usage.

- Alto / alta / alti / alte — tall
- Basso / bassa / bassi / basse — short
- Timido / timida / timidi / timide — shy
- Estroverso / estroversa / estroversi / estroverse — outgoing
- Anziano / anziana / anziani / anziane — elderly
- Gentile / gentili — kind
- Paziente / pazienti — patient
- I capelli — hair (plural in Italian)
- Castani — brown, describing hair
- Ricci — curly
- Gli occhi — eyes
- Verdi — green
- Azzurri — blue, describing eyes
- Blu — blue; this adjective stays unchanged

Anna ha i capelli ricci: Anna has curly hair. Le sedie sono blu: the chairs are blue. Place these descriptive adjectives after the noun in this sample; do not generalise adjective-position changes to every adjective.
