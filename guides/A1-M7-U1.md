## A1-M7-U1 Food and ordering

Goal: Ask for food and drink, talk about an unspecified amount, and use simple quantities.

Prerequisites: Articles and plurals, prendere, mangiare, bere, piace, articulated prepositions. Polite vorrei is introduced in U3; this unit uses other known ordering patterns first.

### Vocabulary preview

Introduce the vocabulary in the following small groups. A noun listed as a mass noun is practised as an amount rather than a countable item here.

- Pass — Active items — Meaning
- 1 drinks — il caffè, il tè, il latte — coffee, tea, milk; already introduced
- 1 drinks — l'acqua, feminine, mass; il vino, mass; la birra → le birre; il cappuccino → i cappuccini — water; wine; beer; cappuccino
- 1 modifiers — naturale; frizzante; caldo/calda; freddo/fredda — still, for water; sparkling; hot; cold
- 2 food — il pane, mass; la pizza → le pizze; la pasta, mass; il panino → i panini — bread, already introduced; pizza; pasta; sandwich roll
- 2 food — il cornetto → i cornetti; il gelato → i gelati; la mela → le mele — croissant-like pastry; ice cream; apple
- 3 partitive examples — lo zucchero, mass; il biscotto → i biscotti; gli spaghetti, masculine plural — sugar; biscuit; spaghetti
- 4 quantities — il bicchiere → i bicchieri; la bottiglia → le bottiglie; il chilo → i chili; il grammo → i grammi — glass; bottle; kilogram; gram

- Active transaction language: il conto → i conti, bill
- Il conto, per favore, the bill, please. Mezzo/mezza, half, agrees with the unit: mezzo chilo. Cento is already introduced
- duecento, trecento, quattrocento, cinquecento, seicento, settecento, ottocento, novecento are the active hundreds for quantity and price practice. Begin with cento and duecento, then expand.

Recognition food extensions: il succo di frutta, fruit juice; la brioche, a pastry whose usage varies by region; il tramezzino, triangular sandwich; il risotto, risotto; la carne, meat; il pesce, fish; la verdura, vegetables as a category; la frutta, fruit as a category; il dolce, dessert. Appendix B supplies extension forms.

### Pass 1 Ordering with known patterns

Prendo un caffè, I'll have a coffee, is a normal ordering use of prendere. Un cappuccino, per favore is also a useful noun-phrase request. Posso avere un bicchiere d'acqua?, may I have a glass of water?, becomes available after the quantity pass below.

Adjectives follow the noun in these models: acqua naturale, still water; acqua frizzante, sparkling water; un tè caldo, a hot tea; una birra fredda, a cold beer. Naturale/frizzante have plural naturali/frizzanti if needed later.

### Pass 2 Food and amounts

Partitive articles express an unspecified amount or a plural “some”. Their forms look like di + definite article:

- Form — Example — Meaning
- del — del pane — some bread
- dello — dello zucchero — some sugar
- dell' — dell'acqua — some water
- della — della pasta — some pasta
- dei — dei biscotti — some biscuits
- degli — degli spaghetti — some spaghetti
- delle — delle mele — some apples

Introduce del/della/dell' first; add the other rows after the associated vocabulary preview. Mangio della pasta, I am eating some pasta. Compro is introduced in U2, so purchase examples with partitives wait until then.

A definite article and a partitive serve different purposes: Mi piace il pane, I like bread generally; Prendo del pane, I'll have some bread. Do not translate every English “some/any” mechanically with a partitive. Italian can also omit it, especially in negatives and general statements. The explicitly introduced pattern Non bevo vino, I do not drink wine, uses a bare mass noun. Non mangio carne is recognition only until carne is activated.

### Pass 3 Quantities

Use quantity/container + di + noun, normally without a definite article for a general substance:

- un bicchiere di vino, a glass of wine;
- una bottiglia d'acqua, a bottle of water;
- un chilo di mele, a kilogram of apples;
- mezzo chilo di pane, half a kilogram of bread;
- duecento grammi di pasta, two hundred grams of pasta.

D'acqua is the contracted di + acqua, not the partitive dell'acqua. You can also say di acqua. A measured quantity already supplies the amount; do not add a partitive in these models.

### Examples and communication

- Prendo un panino e un caffè. I'll have a sandwich and a coffee.
- Mi piacciono le mele. I like apples.
- Posso avere una bottiglia d'acqua? May I have a bottle of water?

— Prendi un caffè?
— No, grazie. Prendo un tè caldo.
— E un cornetto?
— Sì, grazie.

E un cornetto?, and a croissant?, is an ordinary shortened question using known words; no new verb form is implied as a production requirement.

Quick reference: General liking: definite article. Unspecified amount: partitive where natural. Measured amount: quantity + di.

### Ready for audio practice

Use drinks first, then a small food set, then partitives and quantities in separate passes. Add new food nouns only after their previews. Polite conditional forms wait for U3.

- Type — Prompt — Model
- Core — I'll have a coffee and a sandwich. — Prendo un caffè e un panino.
- Variation — May I have a glass of water? — Posso avere un bicchiere d'acqua?
- Integration — My sister likes pasta, but she wants to eat a pizza today. — A mia sorella piace la pasta, ma oggi vuole mangiare una pizza.
