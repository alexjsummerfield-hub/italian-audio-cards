# A2-M2-U2 · Comparisons and superlatives

Complete the earlier A0/A1 units and preceding A2 units before cumulative practice. This is an A2 testing sample, not a complete A2 syllabus.

## Preparation

Compare two people or things with più/meno + adjective + di. Combine di with a definite article where required: di + la = della; di + il = del. Use come for the equality pattern introduced here. Quello means that one, masculine singular, in this sample.

- Più alto di — taller than
- Meno caro di — less expensive than
- Grande → più grande — big → bigger
- Alta come — as tall as
- Migliore di — better than
- Più di dieci — more than ten
- La ragazza → le ragazze — girl → girls
- La classe → le classi — class → classes
- Quello — that one (masculine singular)

Relative superlative: Anna è la ragazza più alta della classe — Anna is the tallest girl in the class. The adjective agrees with the person. Absolute superlatives emphasise degree: buono → buonissimo, buona → buonissima; caro/cara/cari/care → carissimo/carissima/carissimi/carissime; bello/bella → bellissimo/bellissima. Buonissima can mean very good or delicious in a food context. Other natural very good forms include molto buona. This unit practises only these introduced patterns; comparing adjectives/infinitives with che is outside this bank.
