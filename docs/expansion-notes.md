# A0–A2 testing bank · October 2026

384 cards across 28 units: A0 24 cards / 2 units; A1 264 cards / 18 units; A2 96 cards / 8 units. This is a representative testing bank across these topics, not a certification syllabus or a claim of complete A2 coverage.

## Sources

A0/A1 follows the completed Italian A0 and A1 Companion Guide v1.0 (AF-LCS v1.0 / Italian Course Blueprint V3), produced in the earlier Create Italian A0/A1 Companion Guide chat. Its 20 units, vocabulary previews and grammar progression supply the unit preparation pages. Tables are converted into readable rows; recognition and active labels are retained. The 48 original prototype cards retain their IDs and wording. Their existing preparation is included in the expanded avere guide.

A2 uses the relevant Italiano Completo V2 sections as a syllabus source, with new explicit preparation for this finite sample: completed events with avere and essere, imperfetto and a first contrast, description, comparisons, future, past modals and health expressions. This does not add a full pronoun/command curriculum or all of A2 vocabulary. A2 entries are labelled testing samples in the app.

The older source's blanket claim that avere with an essere-infinitive after a past modal is non-standard has not been retained. The relevant alternative is accepted here. Reference: https://www.treccani.it/enciclopedia/avere-o-essere_(La-grammatica-italiana)/

## Editorial decisions

- Direct English sentences only; necessary number/gender/register qualifications are separate, silent notes.
- No sentence-level transformation instructions.
- A0 sounds are model-and-repeat, not vocabulary retrieval; ordinary greeting chunks use the answer field.
- New cards keep stable unit-based IDs. Existing review records and exposure history remain usable.
- Each unit has preparation text. Selected-unit practice uses that unit's bank; cumulative practice uses only that unit and earlier ones in catalogue order. Selecting a later unit assumes its prerequisites have been read. No automatic unlock/certification claim.
- Optional introductory models are only included where that bank has models. A session uses up to the chosen number of available production cards; model-only sound practice uses its 12 models.
- Common explicit-subject answers, selected natural alternatives, and digit versions of number/time/age answers are stored. Alternatives are not exhaustive; the learner can still use Accept my answer.
- The original sample's false a-casa tags on una casa noun phrases have been corrected without changing sentence IDs.
- Bank content is internally curated and checked, not externally native-speaker approved. More real-world language review may identify useful alternatives or refinements.

## Files

catalog.json defines ordered units, guides and bank files. cards.json remains the original 48-card bank; banks/ contains the additions. guides/ contains per-unit preparation. course.js limits session eligibility; scheduler.js retains the review rules. Tests verify source counts, IDs, alternatives, course boundaries and session sizes.
