# A1–M2–U2 · Preparation

Read this before practice. These are the words and patterns used in the sample cards.

## Avere — to have

- Io ho — I have
- Tu hai — you have (one person, informal)
- Lui ha — he has
- Lei ha — she has
- Noi abbiamo — we have
- Voi avete — you have (several people)
- Loro hanno — they have

Italian usually omits the subject pronoun: Ho un libro means I have a book. The h in avere forms is silent.

### Negatives and questions

- Non ho un libro. — I do not have a book.
- Hai un libro? — Do you have a book?

Put non before the verb. Yes/no questions keep the same word order, with rising intonation. Formal Lei is not practised in this sample.

## Everyday nouns

- Un libro — a book
- Due libri — two books
- Una macchina — a car
- Due macchine — two cars
- Una casa — a house
- Due case — two houses

## Family

- Un fratello — a brother
- Due fratelli — two brothers
- Una sorella — a sister
- Due sorelle — two sisters
- Un figlio — a child / a son
- Due figli — two children / two sons
- Hai figli? — Do you have children?

## Quantities and time

- Due — two
- Tre — three
- Tempo — time
- Ho tempo. — I have time.
- Hai tempo? — Do you have time?
- Non ho tempo. — I do not have time.

These expressions with tempo do not use an article.

## Essere — to be

- Io sono — I am
- Tu sei — you are (one person, informal)
- Lui / lei è — he / she is
- Noi siamo — we are
- Voi siete — you are (several people)
- Loro sono — they are
- Non sono italiano. — I am not Italian (a man speaking).

### Useful words and chunks

- A casa — at home
- Siamo a casa. — We are at home.
- E — and
- È — is

## Articles and plurals

- Il libro — the book
- La casa — the house
- Un libro — a book
- Una casa — a house

In these examples, masculine noun plurals end in -i and feminine noun plurals end in -e.

## Adjectives

- Grande — big
- Piccolo / piccola — small (masculine / feminine)
- Nuovo / nuova — new (masculine / feminine)
- Italiano / italiana — Italian (masculine / feminine singular)
- Italiani / italiane — Italian (masculine / feminine plural)

Match the noun's gender and number. Adjectives follow nouns in these examples.

- Una casa piccola — a small house
- Un libro nuovo — a new book
- Due libri italiani — two Italian books

Marco and Anna are names.
