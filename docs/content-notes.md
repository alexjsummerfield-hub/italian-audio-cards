# Content notes

48 curated cards: 7 MODEL, 7 CORE, 18 VARIATION, 6 TRANSFORMATION, 6 INTEGRATION, 4 REVIEW. Editorial approval is for this sample, not an external Italian-language review.

The full companion guide/blueprint was not available as a source file. The preparation guide is therefore the explicit prerequisite contract for this standalone sample. No possessive adjectives, modal verbs, past tenses or unexplained idioms are required. Possession means having, expressed with avere. Review is limited to essere, adjective agreement and the introduced chunk a casa.

Subject pronouns may be included or omitted; lui/lei in model answers clarify the English subject. Figli means children or sons depending on context. No speech recognition or automatic marking. Accepted answers are illustrative, not an exhaustive grading key.

First practice: all seven forms modeled before 20 production cards, weighted toward CORE. Later practice: 20 cards favoring VARIATION, TRANSFORMATION and cumulative material. Session length counts production cards; first-practice models are extra. Timing and composition are prototype defaults to refine by listening.

## Prompt wording

Routine instructions such as Listen & Repeat belong in the card heading, never in the spoken prompt. Optional `prompt_note` is displayed after the sentence in brackets and is not spoken. Use it only when the sentence/context does not resolve number, register or speaker gender; bare English you still needs disambiguation. Where a group is named in the sentence, do not add a redundant plural note. Transformation instructions remain spoken because they define the task. Keep formal address out of this sample as stated in preparation.
